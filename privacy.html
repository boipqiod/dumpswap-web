PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: 2026.05.04

Welcome to DUMPSWAP. Your privacy is not just a feature; it is our fundamental philosophy. This app is designed to be a "Pure Mono" experience—minimalist, functional, and completely private.

01. DATA COLLECTION & STORAGE

DUMPSWAP is a serverless application. We DO NOT collect, store, or share any of your personal data on external servers.

  • Photos: All photos you swap are transmitted directly between devices and stored only in your local device storage.
  • Account: There are no accounts or registrations. We do not know who you are.
  • Metadata: Session logs (who you swapped with and when) remain strictly on your device.

02. PEER-TO-PEER TRANSMISSION

The core of DUMPSWAP is the Peer-to-Peer (P2P) exchange. We use Apple's Multipeer Connectivity framework to facilitate direct communication between devices via:

  • Local Wi-Fi Networks
  • Bluetooth
  • Peer-to-Peer Wi-Fi

Data transmitted during a swap is encrypted by the underlying iOS system frameworks and is never intercepted or stored by DUMPSWAP.

03. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

To provide its core functionality, the app requires the following permissions:

  • Photo Library: To allow you to select a "dump" photo and save received photos.
  • Local Network: To discover and connect to nearby friends.
  • Bluetooth: For low-energy peer discovery when Wi-Fi is unavailable.

04. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

DUMPSWAP does NOT use any third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising platforms (No Google Analytics, No Meta SDK, No AdMob).

05. DATA DELETION

Since all data is stored locally, you have total control. Deleting the application or clearing the history within the app settings will PERMANENTLY erase all associated data. This action cannot be undone.
